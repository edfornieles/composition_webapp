# Family V1 Content Guide

Last updated: April 2026

This document defines the first associative/generic sourcing pass for the Family V1 installation:

- Father (late 40s)
- Mother (late 40s)
- Son (18)
- Sister (16)
- Sister (12)

Goal: build broad content pools that map to character + emotional state + theme tags, without locking into literal narrative too early.

## Character Content Targets

### Father (late 40s)

- **Image motifs**: commute interiors, office fluorescents, receipts, dashboard lights, suit fabric, kitchen table at night, unopened mail, garage shelves, TV glow, half-finished DIY, treadmill stats, sports radio textures.
- **Body-language cues**: stillness, jaw tension, distant stare, repetitive routines, carrying bags, waiting in doorways.
- **Emotional palette**: duty, repression, fatigue, controlled anger, private collapse.
- **Environment textures**: beige interiors, weathered leather, gray-blue dawn, sodium streetlights.

### Mother (late 40s)

- **Image motifs**: domestic choreography, medicine cabinets, laundry cycles, shopping aisles, notes on fridge, mirrors, beauty traces, car park transitions, nighttime scrolling, family photos in frames, plants/wilting flowers.
- **Body-language cues**: multitasking hands, interrupted movement, composed face under strain, quiet rituals.
- **Emotional palette**: maintenance, vigilance, care burden, dissociation, resilience.
- **Environment textures**: warm practical light, tiled surfaces, soft clutter, reflective glass.

### 18-year-old son

- **Image motifs**: gym equipment, locker rooms, gaming glow, bedroom posters, late-night streets, bus stops, cheap takeaway, cracked phone screens, sneakers, skate spots, digital masculinity symbols.
- **Body-language cues**: performance postures, impatience, withdrawal, overconfident front.
- **Emotional palette**: volatility, bravado, shame, hunger for autonomy, impulsive intensity.
- **Environment textures**: neon spill, asphalt, concrete, black-red contrast.

### 16-year-old sister

- **Image motifs**: mirror selfies as concept (not identity), makeup spread, notes/sketchbooks, bedroom LEDs, school corridors, friendship fragments, thrift textures, fandom visuals, public transport solitude.
- **Body-language cues**: performative confidence, private anxiety, fragmented attention, intimacy-distance oscillation.
- **Emotional palette**: social pressure, masking, yearning, identity testing.
- **Environment textures**: pink/blue LED, collage surfaces, hand-written overlays.

### 12-year-old sister

- **Image motifs**: school supplies, stickers, craft debris, playground geometry, cartoons in peripheral screens, bike helmets, lunchboxes, bedrooms-in-transition, toy remnants, family periphery moments.
- **Body-language cues**: observation, mimicry, confusion, bursts of play.
- **Emotional palette**: dependence, sensitivity, curiosity, ambient fear absorption.
- **Environment textures**: brighter tones with sudden shadow intrusions.
- **Ethical rule**: keep all sourcing age-appropriate and non-sexualized.

## Social Account Types to Aggregate From

- Family-lifestyle montage accounts (routines, home transitions, domestic labor fragments).
- Suburban/urban documentary photographers (liminal spaces, evening streets, interiors).
- Micro-vlog day-in-the-life creators (repetition and ritual detail).
- Moodboard/visual-diary accounts (emotion-led image clusters).
- Teen bedroom/desk setup creators (identity through objects).
- Gym/locker-room lifestyle accounts (for son energy field, abstracted).
- School-life aesthetic pages (hallways, stationery, timetables, lockers).
- Domestic organization/cleaning channels (maintenance pressure for parents).
- Commuter POV accounts (cars, trains, office flow, waiting).
- Home renovation/DIY accounts (father strain/control motifs).
- Motherhood/care-work reflection pages (labor/invisible work motifs).
- Craft/DIY youth channels (youngest child textures, safe contexts).
- Weather/season local accounts (ambient emotional modulation).
- Found-footage / VHS aesthetic archives (memory/aftershock states).
- CCTV/public-space observational feeds (distance/paranoia textures).
- Minimal interior photography (numb/baseline states).
- Noise/art-loop creators (fracture/aftershock overlays).

## Curation Workflow (V1)

1. Ingest broad pool by account type + tag family.
2. Curate into composition bins by `character x state x theme`.
3. Keep shared assets in a cross-character reserve pool.
4. Move to specific character narrowing only after broad pools are stable.

## Production Rule

Every composition should have:

1. One primary character/entity
2. One primary emotional state
3. Two to five theme tags

This ensures the system remains reusable while still producing clear family-specific behavior.
